About

The part of cybersecurity I like most is the investigation side of it. I like looking at an alert or a system, figuring out what happened, and understanding why it happened instead of just seeing that something was flagged.

Most of my hands-on work has been with SIEM monitoring, Nmap, Wireshark, Linux and Windows security, vulnerability scanning, incident response, and penetration testing labs.

I made this portfolio to keep track of the work I’ve done and to show what I actually know how to do. I’ll keep adding to it as I work through more labs, projects, and certifications.
