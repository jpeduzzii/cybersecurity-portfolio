01 / About

Security-minded. Hands-on. Always learning.

As a current Cybersecurity analyst working for TekSystems, I have been able to showcase the skills that I built throughout college.

I worked as the Blue Team Lead for the Center for Intelligence, Research, and Training (CIRAT) at Mercyhurst University. In this role, I led seven different analysts by overseeing the operational and information security environment in the lab space. I've written critical documentation, triaged alerts, led meetings, and developed employee training modules.

During my Junior year of school, I was an Information Security Intern at CNB Bank. In this role, I focused heavily on sensitive data remediation, investigating phishing emails, meeting with upper management, and shadowed other employees.

My goal is to continue strengthening my detection, incident response, network analysis, system hardening, and defensive security skills while building a portfolio that demonstrates what I can actually do.
